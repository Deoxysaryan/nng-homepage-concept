Before you enquire

The questions people usually ask before a first consultation.

Ask something else
Where should I begin?

Begin with an enquiry: a WhatsApp message, a call, or your number on the contact page so the team can call you. They explain the options, fees and next steps before you book.

How is a consultation different from the program?

A consultation is a focused conversation around a question or a concern. The Personalised Hand Holding Program extends the guidance over six months, with calls and messages as you work through different areas of life.

Do I need to know which service I want?

No. You can start with the question that is on your mind. Explain what you have been experiencing and what you have already tried. The right approach can be discussed from there.

Can I enquire if I live outside India?

Yes. Mention your country and time zone when you enquire so the team can arrange an online consultation that suits you.

What are the consultation and program fees?

Please ask for the current fees when you enquire. The consultation and the six-month program are separate options, and the team explains the scope and the fee before you decide.

Does this replace medical or professional advice?

No. This is personal and spiritual guidance. It does not replace medical care, mental health treatment, legal advice or financial advice from qualified professionals.
